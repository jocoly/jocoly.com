jocoly.com landing scene asset sources

Rubber duck base mesh: Ryan King Art, Rubber Duck (CC0)
https://ryankingart.gumroad.com/l/duck
Modified in Blender for the interactive duck scene.

Earth surface: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, Blue Marble
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3615

Moon surface: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720

International Space Station 3D model: NASA/Ames Research Center
https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/international-space-station-iss-a/
Converted from NASA's GLB to an uncompressed browser GLB with Blender.

The Sun texture, orbit geometry, shader effects, and space background are generated for this site.

Lo-Fly game asset notices are in /CREDITS.txt and /licenses/.

Scenes audio
Cricket recording (The Campsite): "Campagne la nuit #4", BigSoundBank (CC0, public domain),
trimmed to a 60-second seamless loop and high-passed.
https://bigsoundbank.com/campagne-la-nuit-4-s1880.html
Nylon guitar notes (The Campsite): twelve single notes from the FluidR3_GM soundfont, "acoustic_guitar_nylon",
as converted by the midi-js-soundfonts project. FluidR3_GM is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
Campfire (The Campsite): "Big Branching Fire #3", BigSoundBank (CC0, public domain), trimmed to a seamless loop and levelled.
https://bigsoundbank.com/big-branching-fire-3-s0989.html
Coyote howls (The Campsite): "coyote.wav" by stomachache (CC0), https://freesound.org/people/stomachache/sounds/53256/
and "Coyotes, dog-like animals howling afar, in the background 01" by Simonus18 (CC0), https://freesound.org/people/Simonus18/sounds/855575/
Each is trimmed to a single howl and filtered.
Steam train (The Campsite): "Bigboy 4014 Steam Excursion Train" by Ratspeed (CC0), https://freesound.org/people/Ratspeed/sounds/746322/
A 28 second stretch, made mono and softened.
Everything else in the scene sounds is synthesized in the browser.
